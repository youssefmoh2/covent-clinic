Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a consultation?
+

Book online, call us, or message on WhatsApp. We offer flexible appointment times including evenings.

Are the treatments safe?
+

All treatments are performed by certified medical professionals using FDA-approved products. We discuss all risks during your consultation.

How long do Botox and filler results last?
+

Botox typically lasts 3–6 months; fillers 9–18 months depending on product and area. Maintenance sessions prolong results.

Is there downtime after treatment?
+

Most treatments require little to no downtime. Mild redness may occur but typically resolves within 24–48 hours.

What makes Covent Clinic different in Dubai?
+

Internationally trained specialists, personalised plans, and a luxury patient experience delivering naturally beautiful, long-lasting results.

Do you offer packages or membership plans?
+

Yes — curated treatment packages and a premium membership for year-round maintenance. Contact us for details.
